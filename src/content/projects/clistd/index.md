---
title: "clistd"
description: "A framework- and language-neutral toolkit for documenting, linting, and detecting breaking changes in command-line interfaces."
date: "Oct 17 2026"
repoURL: "https://github.com/matthyk/clistd"
---

- A toolkit for turning command-line interfaces into validated, machine-readable contracts.
- Supports linting CLI definitions with built-in rules and comparing releases to detect breaking changes.
- Includes adapters for Oclif and Commander.js, while keeping the contract and adapter protocol language-neutral.
